# Slovakia — National Council election of 30 September 2023 (national level)

One election: electoral lists with their numbers, votes, truncated published shares and seats; voters on the rolls, voters who took part, valid votes and turnout. Source: minutes of the State Commission for Elections and Control of Financing of Political Parties, on the Interior Ministry site. Rules: Act 180/2014 Coll., version of 1 March 2023.

Rights basis: Copyright Act § 5(a) (information) and § 5(b) (official decisions). This is a reading of the statute, not a statement by the State Commission, and it is weaker than a licence. National figures only.

A list's kind is given only where the minutes state it, for lists that passed a threshold; 18 lists have no established kind. A coalition's votes and seats belong to the coalition.

continuous_party_series is 0: one election cannot form a series.

Not included: 2020, 2016 and 2012 (minutes not located outside statistics.sk), ŠÚ SR files, invalid votes, regional results, candidates and elected deputies.
