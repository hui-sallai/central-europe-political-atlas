# Czechia — Chamber of Deputies election 2025 (national level)

One election, 3–4 October 2025: 26 electoral lists with ČSÚ list codes, list votes, published (truncated) and derived shares, seats; registered voters, issued envelopes and turnout. Source: Český statistický úřad (ČSÚ), DataStat VOLPS202503 and VOLPS03; the national open-data catalogue records no copyright work, no original database, no database maker's right and no personal data for these distributions. Rules: Act 247/1995 Coll. (e-Sbírka informative version).

Not included: list kinds (party, movement or coalition), coalition membership, cross-election party identity, regional results, candidates and preferential votes.
