# Slovenia — National Assembly (Državni zbor) elections

Elections: 2000-10-15, 2004-10-03, 2008-09-21, 2011-12-04, 2014-07-13, 2018-06-03, 2022-04-24, 2026-03-22. Whole chamber (90 seats): 88 deputies from lists of candidates (national figures; seats fixed nationally by D'Hondt among lists with at least 4%) and two national-community deputies (Italian and Hungarian), elected separately from individual candidates. Source: the National Electoral Commission's minutes establishing the result, published in the official gazette (Uradni list RS), cross-checked against the commission's result data file (2011: the commission's report, cross-checked against its final results by polling station; 2004 and 2008: the commission's archived official result pages, the only source read for those years; 2000: the Republic Electoral Commission's report in Uradni list RS 98/2000, read from a page extract of an Internet Archive copy, with two printed percentages that the printed counts do not reproduce kept as text only).

Labels: list names as printed in the minutes; a name containing a person's name is shown by the short name the commission publishes (2000, 2004 and 2011: by the printed name without the person's name, or by row number when the name is a person's name). National-community candidates are stored without names. The National Council (Državni svet) is not a directly elected chamber and is not included. No list is linked to a party or across elections. Constituency-level results are not included in this release.

Reuse basis: official texts are not protected by copyright (ZASP Art. 9), recorded as the owner's reading of the statute, not a licence.
