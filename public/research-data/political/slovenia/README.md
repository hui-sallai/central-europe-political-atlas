# Slovenia — National Assembly (Državni zbor) elections

Elections: 2026-03-22. Whole chamber (90 seats): 88 deputies from lists of candidates (national figures; seats fixed nationally by D'Hondt among lists with at least 4%) and two national-community deputies (Italian and Hungarian), elected separately from individual candidates. Source: the National Electoral Commission's minutes establishing the result, published in the official gazette (Uradni list RS), cross-checked against the commission's result data file.

Labels: list names as printed in the minutes; a name containing a person's name is shown by the short name the commission publishes. National-community candidates are stored without names. The National Council (Državni svet) is not a directly elected chamber and is not included. No list is linked to a party or across elections. Constituency-level results are not included in this release.

Reuse basis: official texts are not protected by copyright (ZASP Art. 9), recorded as the owner's reading of the statute, not a licence.
