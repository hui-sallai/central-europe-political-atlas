# Serbia — National Assembly elections of 2020, 2022, 2023 and 2026

Four elections, whole chamber (250 seats, one nationwide constituency): all electoral lists with votes and seats; voters on the electoral roll, voters who voted, valid and invalid ballots (2022, 2023 and 2026 also voters who turned out). Source: total-results reports of the Republic Electoral Commission (Републичка изборна комисија), for 2022 and 2023 as reproduced in its session notices (sessions 79 and 51); vote shares are over valid ballots; rules from the Law on the Election of Members of Parliament. 2023 was first published from the National Assembly page (Народна скупштина у бројкама), which prints only the seat-winning lists with percentages of voters who voted; it is kept as a historical evidence reference (`src-rs-parlament-brojke-2023`) and agrees with the report. Labels for 2022 and 2023 are in Latin script, as printed on the Latin-script notices. Reuse basis: official materials of state bodies are not copyright works (Copyright Act Art. 6(2)), recorded as a reading of the statute.

List labels: Serbian list titles usually contain personal names. Full titles are not stored. A title that names no person is shown as printed; otherwise only the parties named in it; otherwise no label (row number only). `title_sha256` identifies the printed title. List kinds and national-minority status are not stored, and no list is linked to a party or across elections. Contestant ids are per election and row of the source; 2023 ids follow the RIK report rows (they followed the National Assembly page rows in the earlier publication).
