# Poland — Sejm elections 2011, 2015, 2019 and 2023 (national level)

Four elections: election committees with their list numbers, votes, published shares and seats; eligible voters, ballots, valid and invalid votes and turnout. Source: Państwowa Komisja Wyborcza, result notices published in Dziennik Ustaw (Dz.U. 2011 poz. 1294, 2015 poz. 1731, 2019 poz. 1955, 2023 poz. 2234); validity declared by the Supreme Court (Dz.U. 2011 poz. 1676, 2016 poz. 120, 2020 poz. 9, 2024 poz. 52).

Rights basis: these notices are treated as official documents outside copyright under Art. 4(2) of the Copyright Act. That is a reading of the statute, not a statement by PKW. It covers national figures from these notices only.

Lists belong to election committees, which exist for one election. A coalition committee's votes and seats belong to the coalition. Its members are not stated in these notices and are not derived from its name.

Party identity: a party committee name contains the registered party name or abbreviation (Electoral Code Art. 92 § 1), and PKW communiqués in Monitor Polski print each party's register number (EwP). A verified link in party_identity_links needs the same EwP. A party in a coalition has no figure for that election. Other committees printed with the same name are links with status unresolved: a shared name is not evidence of one legal party. The latest communiqué read covers 2021.

Not included: district or polling-station results, candidates and elected deputies.
