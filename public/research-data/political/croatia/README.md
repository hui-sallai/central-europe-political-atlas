# Croatia — Croatian Parliament (Hrvatski sabor) elections

Elections: 1992-08-02, 1993-02-07, 1995-10-29, 2000-01-03, 2003-11-23, 2007-11-25, 2011-12-04, 2015-11-08, 2016-09-11, 2020-07-05, 2024-04-17. Whole chamber in every election (1992: 138, 1993: 63, 1995: 127, 2000: 151, 2003: 152, 2007: 153, 2011: 151, 2015: 151, 2016: 151, 2020: 151, 2024: 151 seats): ten territorial constituencies (14 seats each), one constituency abroad (3 seats from 2011; in 2000, 2003 and 2007 the number was set by a non-fixed quota printed in the decision: 6, 4, 5 seats) and the national-minority elections in constituency XII (8 seats; 5 in 2000). 1993 elected the Chamber of Counties (Županijski dom), a separate house: 63 members, 3 in each of 21 counties by D'Hondt among county lists (`geography` hr-zupanija-01 … hr-zupanija-21; election `el-hr-zupanijski-dom-1993-02-07`, legislature `lg-hr-zupanijski-dom-1993`); its seats are never added to the Chamber of Representatives, and list leaders are not stored. 1992 (Chamber of Representatives only, 138 seats) used a mixed system: 60 seats from national lists (D'Hondt; a 3% threshold reproduces them), 60 single-member constituencies (most votes), 4 national-minority special constituencies, and 14 members declared elected from national lists (13 for the Serbian community, 1 for minorities under 8%; `members_declared_elected_from_list`); every 1992 percentage is printed on the voters who voted, invalid ballots included (`share_base`) (`geography` hr-national-list, hr-smd-01 … hr-smd-60, hr-special-1 … hr-special-4). 1995 (Chamber of Representatives only, 127 seats) used a mixed system: 80 seats from national lists (D'Hondt, 5%), 28 single-member constituencies (most votes), 12 seats from special lists for voters abroad and 7 national-minority seats in five special constituencies (`geography` hr-national-list, hr-smd-01 … hr-smd-28, hr-special-list-abroad, hr-special-1 … hr-special-5); single-member candidates are shown by their nominating parties and row number, never by name. Source: final-results decisions of the State Electoral Commission (Državno izborno povjerenstvo), one per constituency and per minority election, identified by KLASA/URBROJ and file checksum in `political_source_registry`.

Units: results are per constituency (`geography` hr-ie-01 … hr-ie-11, hr-ie-12-<minority>). The same party may stand in different coalitions in different constituencies, so list votes are not added into national vote shares. `legislatures.seats_by_identical_list_title` adds seats only across lists with the same printed title. Turnout is printed per decision (the 2000 decisions print turnout counts without percentages, so 2000 turnout is derived); `hr-territorial-i-x` sums constituencies I–X and, for 2024, matches the commission’s archive table.

Labels: lists are stored with the party names printed in the decision. Party names containing a living person’s name are shown by the abbreviation printed with them, or omitted (row number only when nothing remains); independent lists printed with their leader’s name (2000–2007) are shown as NEOVISNA LISTA; `title_sha256` identifies the full printed title. National-minority candidates are stored without names or nominators (row number, votes, elected). No list is linked to a party or across elections.

Reuse basis: official texts of state bodies are not protected by copyright (Croatian Copyright Act), recorded as the owner’s reading of the statute, not a licence.
