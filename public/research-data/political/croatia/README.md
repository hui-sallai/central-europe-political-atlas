# Croatia — Croatian Parliament (Hrvatski sabor) elections

Elections: 2000-01-03, 2003-11-23, 2007-11-25, 2011-12-04, 2015-11-08, 2016-09-11, 2020-07-05, 2024-04-17. Whole chamber in every election (2000: 151, 2003: 152, 2007: 153, 2011: 151, 2015: 151, 2016: 151, 2020: 151, 2024: 151 seats): ten territorial constituencies (14 seats each), one constituency abroad (3 seats from 2011; in 2000, 2003 and 2007 the number was set by a non-fixed quota printed in the decision: 6, 4, 5 seats) and the national-minority elections in constituency XII (8 seats; 5 in 2000). Source: final-results decisions of the State Electoral Commission (Državno izborno povjerenstvo), one per constituency and per minority election, identified by KLASA/URBROJ and file checksum in `political_source_registry`.

Units: results are per constituency (`geography` hr-ie-01 … hr-ie-11, hr-ie-12-<minority>). The same party may stand in different coalitions in different constituencies, so list votes are not added into national vote shares. `legislatures.seats_by_identical_list_title` adds seats only across lists with the same printed title. Turnout is printed per decision (the 2000 decisions print turnout counts without percentages, so 2000 turnout is derived); `hr-territorial-i-x` sums constituencies I–X and, for 2024, matches the commission’s archive table.

Labels: lists are stored with the party names printed in the decision. Party names containing a living person’s name are shown by the abbreviation printed with them, or omitted (row number only when nothing remains); independent lists printed with their leader’s name (2000–2007) are shown as NEOVISNA LISTA; `title_sha256` identifies the full printed title. National-minority candidates are stored without names or nominators (row number, votes, elected). No list is linked to a party or across elections.

Reuse basis: official texts of state bodies are not protected by copyright (Croatian Copyright Act), recorded as the owner’s reading of the statute, not a licence.
