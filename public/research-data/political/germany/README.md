# Germany National Elections 1990–2025

Ten national Bundestag elections. 2002–2025 (Slice 1A): second votes only. 1990, 1994 and 1998 (owner-approved additive extension, 2026-11-23): first and second votes as separate ballots (vote_type first_vote / second_vote, never added together), final seats split into constituency and Land-list seats, overhang seats, the 656 statutory seats, Hare/Niemeyer seat reproduction and the legal basis of each election (law titles, adoption and promulgation dates, Federal Law Gazette references; entry-into-force dates are not asserted). 1990: the 5% threshold applied separately to the two electoral areas (former Federal territory with West Berlin; new Länder with East Berlin); the two areas' second votes are included. Publisher aggregates ("Sonstige") are not contestants; the first-vote aggregate of voter groups and individual candidates is one aggregate row. CDU and CSU, and in 1990 GRÜNE and B90/Gr., are separate election-specific contestants; no party continuity is asserted.
