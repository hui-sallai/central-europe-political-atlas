# Hungary — National Assembly elections of 2022 and 2026 (national list tier)

Two elections: party lists and nationality lists with their numbers, votes and national list seats; voters on all registers, voters who took part, and ballot counts. Source: minutes of the National Election Commission on the national list result, transcribed from scanned PDFs identified by checksum. Rules: Act CCIII of 2011.

Coverage: these figures cover the national list tier only, 93 of 199 seats. Seats and votes in the 106 single-member districts are not included, so nothing here shows which list won the most seats overall.

Denominators: party-list and nationality-list votes are separate ballots, each with its own total. `derived_vote_share` is computed within one ballot. The election office's application prints shares over both ballots together, so its figures differ. `surplus_votes` and `votes_for_list_seat_allocation` are the seat-allocation inputs in the minutes and are never added to `votes`. Turnout is computed from the counts in the minutes, because they print no percentage.

Rights basis: Copyright Act (Act LXXVI of 1999) § 1(4), official notices and case documents, for minutes whose content the National Election Office must publish under Act XXXVI of 2013 § 76(2)(e). This is a reading of the statute accepted by the owner, not a statement by the office, and no licence exists. The office's Impresszum is on a host that disallows robots and was not read. National figures only.

A joint list's votes and seats belong to the joint list. The list kind comes from the printed name and the official application; for 2022 no official joint-list label was read.

continuous_party_series is 0: no party register was reviewed, so no link is made between the two elections, even where a name repeats.

Not included: 2018 and earlier, constituency results, county results, candidates, elected deputies, and the application's CSV.
