# Hungary — National Assembly elections of 2002, 2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022 and 2026

## 2002 and 2006 (Act XXXIV of 1989 as amended in 1994 and 1997)

386 seats in two rounds (7 and 21 April 2002; 9 and 23 April 2006) and three tiers, as in 2010. By statute 176 single-member, up to 152 territorial-list and at least 58 national compensation seats; the final allocation was 176 + 140 + 70 in 2002 (12 territorial seats passed to the national lists) and 176 + 146 + 64 in 2006 (6 passed). The same three ballots are kept apart, each with its own total: territorial-list votes (round 1; `hu02_territorial_list_vote`, `hu06_territorial_list_vote`) and national sums of single-member candidate votes per nominating organisation in round 1 and round 2 (`hu02_smc_round1_vote`, `hu02_smc_round2_vote`, `hu06_smc_round1_vote`, `hu06_smc_round2_vote`; votes only). Round-1 single-member seats per organisation come from the round-1 composition page; round-2 seats are the final seats minus round-1 seats (`round2_seats_derived`). Seats won outside the lists are in `constituency_seats_outside_lists`: 2002, one seat of joint MSZP-SZDSZ candidates; 2006, six seats of joint MSZP-SZDSZ candidates and one of SOMOGYÉRT candidates. Joint lists (FIDESZ-MDF in 2002, FIDESZ-KDNP in 2006) stay whole; the threshold page halves their votes only to test the threshold, and that split is not used. Turnout is the printed round-1 figure (2002: 70.53%; 2006: 67.83%); round 2 (2002: 131 constituencies, 73.51%; 2006: 110 constituencies, 64.39%) is in `round_2`. The national totals pages also print a column that adds single-member and territorial-list votes together; it pools two ballots and is not stored. Rules: Act XXXIV of 1989 read on the legislation database with the amending act of each provision; § 8(5) (thresholds) was set by Act XCVI of 1997, in force 1997-11-05, and § 8(3) and the calculation annex by Act III of 1994, in force 1994-01-20. Sources: the National Election Office's 2002 and 2006 archive pages (round indexes and summaries, threshold, national totals, compensation, composition after each round), read once each by hand; national figures only, no per-constituency rows, no candidate names.

## 2010 (Act XXXIV of 1989)

386 seats in two rounds (11 and 25 April 2010) and three tiers: by statute 176 single-member, up to 152 territorial-list and at least 58 national compensation seats; the final allocation was 176 + 146 + 64 because 6 territorial seats passed to the national lists. Three ballots are kept apart, each with its own total: territorial-list votes (round 1; `hu10_territorial_list_vote`), and national sums of single-member candidate votes per nominating organisation in round 1 and round 2 (`hu10_smc_round1_vote`, `hu10_smc_round2_vote`; votes only, `seats` null). Each territorial-list row holds `territorial_list_seats`, `national_list_seats` (compensation, recomputed by D'Hondt over the printed fragment votes), `constituency_seats` (single-member seats of candidates nominated by the same organisations) and `whole_chamber_seats`. Two single-member seats were won outside the lists (joint FIDESZ-KDNP-MVMP candidates; one independent): see `constituency_seats_outside_lists` in the legislature record. Turnout is the printed round-1 figure (64.38%); round 2 (57 constituencies, 46.66%) is in `round_2`. Sources: the National Election Office's 2010 archive pages (round summaries, threshold, national totals, compensation, composition) and its description of the seat allocation, read once each by hand; national figures only, no per-constituency rows, no candidate names. One single-member organisation label containing a person's name is stored without it.

## 2014–2026 (Act CCIII of 2011)

Four elections under Act CCIII of 2011: party lists and nationality lists with their numbers, votes, national list seats, single-member constituency seats and whole-chamber seats; voters on all registers, voters who took part, and ballot counts. Sources: minutes of the National Election Commission on the national list result, transcribed from PDFs identified by checksum (2018: the minutes printed as the annex to the Kúria's review decisions; 2014: the same minutes published only as an HTML page in minutes form, with the threshold and seat-calculation pages of the same archive, read by hand once); the election office's composition page of each election for constituency seats, read by hand once. Rules: Act CCIII of 2011.

Seat scopes: `seats` are national list seats (`seat_scope` = `national_list_tier`, 93 seats in all). `constituency_seats` are single-member district seats (106 in all). `whole_chamber_seats` = `seats` + `constituency_seats` (199 in all). Divide `seats` by 93 for a list-tier share and `whole_chamber_seats` by 199 for whole-chamber representation; `seats` ÷ 199 describes neither. Every one of the 199 seats is attributed: each composition row is matched to the list of the minutes nominated by the same organisations, its list seats must equal the minutes, and the build fails otherwise. A nationality list holds no constituency seat. In 2018 one constituency seat was won by an independent candidate: it is counted in `independent_constituency_seats` of the legislature record, attributed to no list, and not named.

Denominators: party-list and nationality-list votes are separate ballots, each with its own total. `derived_vote_share` is computed within one ballot. The election office's application prints shares over both ballots together, so its figures differ. `surplus_votes` and `votes_for_list_seat_allocation` are the seat-allocation inputs in the minutes and are never added to `votes`. Constituency votes are not held. Turnout is computed from the counts in the minutes, because they print no percentage.

Rights basis: Copyright Act (Act LXXVI of 1999) § 1(4), official notices and case documents, for minutes whose content the National Election Office must publish under Act XXXVI of 2013 § 76(2)(e), extended by the owner on 2026-11-06 to the office's official composition pages for aggregate seat counts only. This is a reading of the statute accepted by the owner, not a statement by the office, and no licence exists. The office's Impresszum is on a host that disallows robots and was not read. National figures only.

A joint list's votes and seats, including the constituency seats of candidates nominated by the same organisations, belong to the joint list and are never divided among its organisations. The list kind comes from the printed name and the official application; for 2022 no official joint-list label was read.

continuous_party_series is 0: the minutes give each nominating organisation an identifier specific to one election, joint lists are election-specific, and no party register was reviewed, so no link is made between elections, even where a name repeats. 2014 list names are abbreviations as printed; one 2014 list name that begins with a person's name is stored without it (`label_basis`, `printed_label_sha256`).

Not included: 1998 and earlier, constituency votes and per-district results, county results, candidates, elected deputies, and the application's CSV.
