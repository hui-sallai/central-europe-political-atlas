# Austria — Nationalrat elections 2002-11-24, 2006-10-01, 2008-09-28, 2013-09-29, 2017-10-15, 2019-09-29, 2024-09-29

Whole chamber (183 seats), national level. Source: the Federal Ministry of the Interior's final-result workbooks (national row), the Bundeswahlbehörde's announcements under § 108 (6) NRWO (2013, 2017, 2019) or its signed decision (2024), the final seat tables (2006, 2008, 2019, 2024; 2006 also checked against the third-stage table) and, for 2002, the seats row of the legacy .xls workbook, saved by the owner; checksums in `political_source_registry`. Every year: party votes add up to the valid votes, the nine Länder add up to the national row, and national D'Hondt over 183 seats among the parties above 4% reproduces every party's seats. 2006, 2008, 2019 and 2024 also give seats by tier (regional constituencies — 43 before the 2013 reform, 39 after — Länder, federal).

Labels: the official short name (Kurzbezeichnung). Printed party names are stored only when they name no person (otherwise SHA-256 only); a short name that is a person's name is shown by its column position. No candidate names. No party is linked across elections.

Turnout is derived (votes cast ÷ eligible voters). The 2006 and 2008 polling days are printed on the BMI result pages; the 2002, 2013 and 2024 polling days are not printed in the supplied files and come from the national election source registry.

Reuse basis: official works are free of copyright (UrhG § 7), recorded as the owner's reading of the statute, not a licence; the BMI Impressum permits attributed non-commercial reuse.
