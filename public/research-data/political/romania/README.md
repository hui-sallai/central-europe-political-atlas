# Romania — Parliament elections of 11 December 2016, 6 December 2020 and 1 December 2024 (Chamber of Deputies and Senate)

Six separate elections, never combined: each chamber in each year is its own election.

## 2016

Chamber of Deputies (329 seats: 312 list seats and 17 national-minority organisation seats) and Senate (136 seats). Votes are national sums of the Permanent Electoral Authority's polling-station and postal files on data.gov.ro (OGL-ROU-1.0); list seats come from its mandate allocation files; the 17 minority seats follow Law 208/2015 Art. 56 and match the organisations the Chamber of Deputies lists for its 2016–2020 term.

Limits: registered voters, voters and turnout are not published (the AEP count columns are undocumented); the source is the AEP centralisation, not the Central Electoral Bureau's final act; the Chamber's seat allocation reproduces within one seat under a simplified model (the official file is used).

## 2020

From the Central Electoral Bureau's final acts 1640 (Senate) and 1639 (Chamber of Deputies) of 17 December 2020, read from Internet Archive captures of the publisher PDFs saved by the owner and transcribed by eye from the scans. Chamber of Deputies: 330 seats (312 list seats and 18 national-minority organisation seats, section m.5). Senate: 136 seats. National D'Hondt over the printed remainders reproduces the national stage (section j) exactly. The polling day (6 December 2020) is printed on the bureau's results page, not in the acts. One list name containing persons' surnames is shown without them.

## 2024

From the Central Electoral Bureau's final acts 2RF (Senate) and 3RF (Chamber of Deputies) of 7 December 2024, saved by the owner and transcribed by eye from the scans. Chamber of Deputies: 331 seats (312 list seats and 19 national-minority organisation seats, section m.5). Senate: 134 senators elected of 136 seats; the act deploys two national-stage seats to no constituency, so Giurgiu and Ialomița each keep one slot unfilled, and states no reason. Registered voters, voters, valid and null votes are printed in the act; turnout is derived (b ÷ a). National D'Hondt over the printed remainders reproduces the national stage (section j).

Limits (2020 and 2024): the polling day is not printed in the result sections; constituency votes are not in the acts; candidate names are never transcribed.

All years: independent candidates are aggregated without names; no list is linked to a party or across elections.
