# Romania — Parliament elections of 11 December 2016 (Chamber of Deputies and Senate)

Two separate elections, never combined: Chamber of Deputies (329 seats: 312 list seats and 17 national-minority organisation seats) and Senate (136 seats). Votes are national sums of the Permanent Electoral Authority's polling-station and postal files on data.gov.ro (OGL-ROU-1.0); list seats come from its mandate allocation files; the 17 minority seats follow Law 208/2015 Art. 56 and match the organisations the Chamber of Deputies lists for its 2016–2020 term.

Limits: registered voters, voters and turnout are not published (the AEP count columns are undocumented); the source is the AEP centralisation, not the Central Electoral Bureau's final act; independent candidates are aggregated without names; the Chamber's seat allocation reproduces within one seat under a simplified model (the official file is used). No list is linked to a party or across elections.
