Changes by the Atlas: for the home map, ADM1 geometries are simplified (Douglas–Peucker, tolerance 0.025°) and
tagged with their country. Each country feature keeps its own source and licence (feature properties
boundary_source / boundary_license); the file is a collection of separately licensed features, not a merged database.

The regional maps use Eurostat/GISCO NUTS 2024 (© EuroGeographics) for all countries except Poland, whose
administrative layer uses the geoBoundaries ODbL boundaries above (files in /data/boundaries/v086/).
