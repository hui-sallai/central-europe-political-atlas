# Correction and takedown policy

**ATLAS POLICY.** Requests go to the public contact address (Legal and Privacy pages).

1. **Receive:** page, disputed content, reason, verifiable evidence. Do not ask for unnecessary personal data.
2. **Triage (within a reasonable time):** factual error · outdated data · copyright/trademark · personal data · defamation
   or reputation risk · security.
3. **High-risk factual claims** (crime, corruption, illegality, misconduct, extremism, foreign control, financial
   impropriety): if the source, attribution or evidentiary basis is weak, unpublish the item first and review.
4. **Fix:** correct with a dated note; data corrections go through the owning workflow (weekly news, official-data
   refresh, political production) — never a silent edit of canonical stores.
5. **Respond** to the requester with the outcome.
6. **Record:** keep a minimal outcome record (date, page, category, action) without personal content in Git; keep the
   correspondence privately per retention_policy.md.

**Defamation / reputation gate (ATLAS POLICY):** claims about named people or organisations in the categories above must
carry the source, exact attribution (“X said / the prosecutor stated”), a clear fact-versus-analysis distinction and a
correction path. The Atlas publishes no motive or character judgments. Event summaries keep a second boundary paragraph
stating what the record does not conclude.

**ENGINEERING CONTROL:** high-risk keyword events are listed by the legal-security validator report; each must have a source
URL, source name and two-paragraph summary.
