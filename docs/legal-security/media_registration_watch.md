# Media registration watch (Hungary)

**Status: WATCH — no legal conclusion.**

**LAW / EXTERNAL (context).** Hungarian media law (Act CLXXXV of 2010 on media services and mass media, and Act CIV of
2010 on press freedom) sets registration duties for some media services and press products, administered by the NMHH
(National Media and Infocommunications Authority). Whether an independent, non-commercial, static research website falls
within those definitions depends on facts the Atlas has not assessed: for example, economic nature, editorial
responsibility, regularity and the establishment of the operator.

**ATLAS POLICY.**
- No conclusion is drawn here, and the site does not claim to be registered or exempt.
- Triggers for re-review: commercialisation, regular news-style publication beyond the Event Library, advertising, an
  organisational publisher, or an enquiry from an authority.
- The owner decides whether to seek legal advice (OWNER DECISION). No authority is contacted automatically.
