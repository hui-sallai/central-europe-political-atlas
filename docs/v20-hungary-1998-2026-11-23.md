## D. The two semantic differences

**1. Territorial-list votes are a valid-round composite.**
- Round 1 was invalid in Hajdú-Bihar (48.84% took part) and Szabolcs-Szatmár-Bereg (49.58%), because under half the voters took part. This is an invalid *round*, not invalid *ballots*.
- Both districts voted again on 24 May.
- The office set the 5% border only after round 2 ("csak a második forduló után").
- The national total of 4,547,682 is therefore round 1 in 18 districts (4,064,201, published on the round-1 table) plus round 2 in 2 districts (483,481, derived as the difference).
- The derived part lies inside the range implied by the printed district percentages.
- The real round-1 territorial ballot in all 20 districts (valid 4,483,974; invalid 48,401) is a different observation and is kept separately (`actual_round1_ballot`).

**2. The national single-member table apportions joint candidates.**
- Footnote 1 of l22: joint candidates' seats appear under the first-named party, and their votes "a jelöltet indító pártok között felosztva".
- The rows are stored as `source_apportioned_party_total`. Their seats are kept only as `first_named_party_seats_final` / `first_named_party_round1_seats`, never as group seats.
- Original groups are used only where the office prints them:
  - l23a for Fidesz/MDF: separate Fidesz 95 candidates / 55 seats, separate MDF 94 / 2, Fidesz-MDF 52 / 35, MDF-Fidesz 26 / 15.
  - l50, the chamber composition: one joint "Fidesz MDF" row of 50 seats.
- l23a does not print its round. Its votes equal the round-2 column for Fidesz and MDF exactly (1,085,538 + 620,617 = 1,706,155; 22,714 + 334,177 = 356,891), and its seats equal their final seats. It is therefore read as round 2.
- l23a does not show whether other parties had joint candidates. No other original group is reconstructed, and no group is claimed for round 1.

The 2002–2006 report §S no longer says the 2002 builder applies unchanged.

## F. Data contract (typed extension)

`src/lib/politicalRoundScope.ts` holds the types and two pure helpers. Records without the new fields keep their meaning: `single_round` and original nominations.

| Record | New fields | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `vote_definitions[]` | `roundScope`, `rounds`, `attributionBasis`, `geographicScope` | `hu98_territorial_list_valid_round_vote`: `valid_round_composite`, rounds [1, 2], `{territorial_districts: 20, round1_districts: 18, round2_districts: 2}`. Single-member ballots: `single_round`, `source_apportioned_party_total`, 176 / 175 constituencies. |
| `election_turnout[]` (composite) | no `round`; `round_scope`, `round_composition`, `actual_round1_ballot` | `eligible_voters`, `voters`, `published_turnout` and `invalid_votes` are null by design. `turnout_basis` is `unknown`/`unknown`, with a definition explaining why. |
| `election_turnout[]` (single-member) | `geographic_scope`, `round_outcome`, `ballots_in_urn`, `published_invalid_percent_of_voters` | Round 1: 8,062,708 / 4,536,254 / 56.26%, 176 constituencies. Round 2: 8,016,397 / 4,570,386 / 57.01%, 175 constituencies. |
| `election_results[]` (lists) | `round_scope`, `vote_components`, `attribution_basis: original_list`, `published_vote_share` | Printed share (2 decimals, same denominator). `derived_vote_share` is still given. |
| `election_results[]` (single-member) | `attribution_basis`, `first_named_party_seats_final`, `first_named_party_round1_seats`, `nomination_group_components`, `round1_table_votes` | `seats`, `round1_seats` and `single_member_seats_won_final` are deliberately absent. |
| `legislatures[]` | `constituency_seats_outside_lists[].components` | The joint row "Fidesz-MDF / MDF-Fidesz" has 50 seats (35 + 15). The independent has 1. |

Helpers:
- `roundScopeOf(def)` and `attributionOf(def)` return the defaults for older records.
- `singleRoundTurnout(def, row)` returns null for a composite.
- `roundScopeProblems(def, row)` rejects a composite that names a round, carries a turnout, electorate or invalid count, or whose parts do not add up.

What the extension means for each area:
- **Selection:** the composite is a registered ballot with its own id. It is the primary list ballot, as in 2002–2010.
- **Dates:** the composite spans `date`–`date_end`. Its parts carry their own dates.
- **Scope labels:** they come from `roundScope` and `geographicScope`.
- **Comparability:** a composite against a single-round list ballot differs in round basis. Turnout must not be differenced; shares can be shown side by side only with the scope stated.
- **Exports:** JSON and CSV carry the new fields as written; CSV cells hold the objects as JSON.
- **Notebook state:** the URL carries the stable ballot ID. Collected evidence retains polling dates or the composite interval, geographic scope, attribution basis and linked official documents.
- **Frontend:** see §J.

## L. Codex handoff — HUNGARY 1998 ROUND-SCOPE INTEGRATION

Original integration acceptance criteria, retained for traceability. The implementation and verification are recorded in §§H–J.

1. **Composite list ballot** (`roundScope = valid_round_composite`):
   - Never label it round 1 and never give it a turnout, electorate or invalid count.
   - Show its date span and its two parts from `round_composition`: 18 districts in round 1 (published) and 2 districts in round 2 (derived; the two district registers and printed percentages).
   - Show the real round-1 territorial ballot (`actual_round1_ballot`) as a separate observation.
   - Use `roundScopeProblems()` as a guard.

   - Read `singleRoundTurnout()` from the single-member ballots: round 1 is 56.26% (8,062,708 registered; all 176 constituencies; the same voters cast the list ballot), and round 2 is 57.01% (175 constituencies).
   - Do not attach either figure to the list ballot.
   - If a national turnout point is shown for 1998, label it round 1, all constituencies.
3. **Comparability:**
   - A composite and a single-round list ballot (1998 against 2002/2006/2010) differ in round basis. Do not difference turnout.
   - Shares may be shown side by side only with that difference stated.
   - Keep the existing guards for everything else.
4. **Published shares:** 1998 list rows carry `published_vote_share` (two decimals, same valid-round denominator). Show it as published; 2002+ shares remain computed.
5. **Single-member ballots** (`attributionBasis = source_apportioned_party_total`):

   - Never show `first_named_party_*` as group seats.
   - Show `nomination_group_components` for Fidesz and MDF in round 2 (separate and joint groups).
   - Show `round1_table_votes` as a recorded discrepancy.
6. **Chamber:** the outside-list joint holder is "Fidesz-MDF / MDF-Fidesz", 50 seats with `components` 35 + 15; the independent has 1. The 335 list-linked seats already render.
7. **Downloads:**
   - Include `round_scope`, `round_composition`, `actual_round1_ballot`, `attribution_basis`, `vote_components` and `nomination_group_components`.
   - Fill `rounds[]` from the per-ballot rows when the primary ballot is composite.
8. **Thresholds:** the 1998 texts say the 10% and 15% thresholds were in force but not calculated, because no joint list stood. Keep the distinction between "in force" and "relevant".
