Central Europe Political Atlas v2.0. Version, package version and release metadata are unchanged.

## 1. Official sources examined

The owner brief named two pages. Each was read once by hand in the built-in browser on 2026-11-15:

| Election | Page | Act |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 | `https://www.rik.parlament.gov.rs/vest/sr/324925/79-sednica-republicke-izborne-komisije.php` | RIK session 79, 5 July 2022: report on the total results adopted |
| 2023 | `https://www.rik.parlament.gov.rs/vest/sr/564488/51-sednica-republicke-izborne-komisije.php` | RIK session 51, 12 January 2024: total-results report adopted |

How the pages were handled:
- The host `www.rik.parlament.gov.rs` was already registered for manual reading only.
- No script, results application, data endpoint or other page of the site was used.
- Title checksums and the table-text checksums were computed in the browser from each page's own DOM text.
- The page texts are kept in `local-evidence/serbia/2026-11-15/`, which is not committed.
- Both pages are in the site's Latin-script version, so stored labels are in Latin script as printed.
- Both footers state CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 Serbia for the web presentation. This is the same footer already recorded for the 2026 RIK notice.

Voters who turned out and voters who voted are separate printed figures, and both are stored separately. Turnout is derived as voters who voted ÷ voters on the roll: 58.53% for 2022 and 58.69% for 2023. It is labelled as derived.

## 2. 2022 admission decision

**Admitted** under admission `serbia-nsk-2022-2023-rik`, with gate record `docs/political-data/serbia_nsk2022_2023_admission.json` (all 8 gates closed).

What was checked:
- All 19 lists are recorded with votes and seats, and list votes sum exactly to valid ballots.
- Invalid plus valid ballots equal voters who voted.
- Seats total 250 and reproduce exactly under highest quotients with the 3% threshold (of voters who voted, 114,182 votes) and the 35% quotient increase. In 2022 the increase applied to the five below-threshold lists that hold seats.

What is not stored or inferred:
- National-minority status is decided by RIK and is not printed on the page, so it is not stored. The seat recomputation treats below-threshold seat-holders as minority lists, as a consistency check only.
- No list is linked to a party or to a list in another election (`continuous_series_eligible` is false; `party_relation_status` is `not_attributed_no_party_register_reviewed`).

The previous report's conclusion that "2022 is unavailable" is withdrawn: the RIK notice is public and complete.

## 3. 2023 before and after

| | Before (National Assembly page) | After (RIK report) |
|---|---|---|
| Lists | 10 (seat-winning only) | 18 (all) |
| Voters on the roll | not printed | 6,500,666 |
| Voters who voted | 3,815,007 | 3,815,007 |
| Valid and invalid ballots | not printed | 3,710,978 and 104,029 |
| Percentages | printed % of **voters who voted**, truncated, not a vote share | vote share derived over **valid ballots** |
| Turnout | not computable | 58.69% (derived) |
| `result_coverage` | `seat_winning_lists_only` | `all_lists` |

How the earlier source relates to the new one:
- The National Assembly page is kept in the evidence record as `historical_reference` and in the source registry as `src-rs-parlament-brojke-2023`, with role `historical_evidence_reference_not_used_for_published_figures`.
- The build cross-checks it on every run. Each of its 10 lists equals exactly one RIK list in votes and seats, and together they cover all 10 seat-winning RIK lists. Each printed percentage equals votes ÷ voters who voted, truncated to two decimals.
- Those percentages are not published as vote shares. For example, the first list is 46.75% of voters who voted on the Assembly page but 48.07% of valid ballots.

Two consequences of the change:
- **Contestant ids:** 2023 ids (`ct-rs-nsk23-NN`) now follow the RIK report rows. Under the earlier publication they followed the Assembly page rows, so for example `ct-rs-nsk23-06` was the VMSZ list and is now RIK row 6 (0 seats). The README states this.
- **UI coverage sentence:** the "seat-winning lists only" sentence no longer appears. The code path remains for any future partial source.

## 4. New observations and lists

- **2022:** 1 election, 19 lists and their 19 results, 1 turnout row, 1 legislature, 1 electoral system and 1 source-registry entry.
- **2023:** upgraded from 10 to 18 lists and results, now with valid and invalid ballots, voters on the roll, voters who turned out and polling stations. One registry entry was added for the historical reference.
- **2020 and 2026:** every record is identical apart from the evidence and parser checksums in provenance (verified field by field).

List labels follow owner rule (b), unchanged:
- A title naming no person is stored verbatim. These labels are checked against the title checksum (2022 rows 12, 15 and 16; 2023 rows 16 and 18).
- Otherwise only the parties named in the title are stored.
- Otherwise there is no label, and the list is shown as "Изборна листа, ред. бр. N" (2022 rows 1, 2 and 17; 2023 rows 1, 3, 13 and 15).
- Coalition names, slogans, nicknames, a trade union and an association are omitted. Each omission is noted per row in `label_notes`.
- A scan of every Serbian store, export, evidence file and the script for the persons named in the 2022 and 2023 titles finds none.

## 5. Legal and source governance

- **Rights:** same basis as the 2020 and 2026 RIK reports. Copyright Act Art. 6(2) treats official materials of state bodies as not copyright works. This is the owner's reading of the statute, weaker than a licence. Only figures are transcribed, never page text or design, with attribution to RIK, on a non-commercial site.
- **Records:**
  - new gate record;
  - new `POLITICAL_ADMISSIONS` entry `serbia-nsk-2022-2023-rik`;
  - new admission record with exact hashes of all 23 Serbian files;
  - Stage C record: Serbia's elections are now [2020, 2022, 2023, 2026], with a new owner decision;
  - UI amendment `serbia-politics-2022-2023-rik-2026-11-15` covering 5 presentation files, baseline 314dc64.
- **Earlier records:** the gate records for 2020/2026 and for the 2023 Assembly-page admission are hash-pinned and unchanged.
- **Statute:** the 2022 and 2023 elections were held under Law 14/2022 before the 2026 amendment. That earlier text was not read separately, so the seat rule is a consistency check for those years, as for 2020.
- **Frozen files:** Germany, the release metadata, the frozen registries and the model code are unchanged.
