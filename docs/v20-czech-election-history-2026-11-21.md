## Working with the evidence

| Research question | Where to look |
|---|---|
| Which rules applied when a list won seats? | [Compare 2017 and 2021](https://hy-central-europe-analysis.org/en/politics/czechia/?election=el-cz-ps-2017-10-20&compare=el-cz-ps-2021-10-08#politics-compare-title). In 2002–2017 qualified lists received seats through regional successive divisors. In 2021 and 2025 a regional quota was followed by national remainder allocation. The single-list threshold was 5% in both systems. The earlier thresholds for two-, three- and larger coalitions were 10%, 15% and 20%. The later thresholds were 8% for two members and 11% for three or more. The expandable details cite the appropriate statutes for each election. |
| What can be compared numerically? | [Czechia 2010 and 2021](https://hy-central-europe-analysis.org/en/politics/czechia/?election=el-cz-ps-2010-05-28&compare=el-cz-ps-2021-10-08#politics-compare-title). Published turnout uses issued envelopes divided by registered voters in all seven elections. Each chamber had 200 seats. National turnout and contestant counts retain the year of the election. The ten-country comparison identifies the recorded turnout numerator for each national electorate, including actual voters in Germany. |
| What do individual list observations establish? | [Czechia 2002 and 2025](https://hy-central-europe-analysis.org/politics/czechia/?election=el-cz-ps-2002-06-14&compare=el-cz-ps-2025-10-03#politics-compare-title). The 2002–2013 percentages are calculated from list votes divided by valid votes. The 2002 KOALICE list won 31 seats (15.5% of the chamber). The 2006 coalition list won zero seats and is present in the detailed results. Seats and votes belong to the whole ballot entity. Verified party-series links begin in 2017. |
| What is the source for a seat total or legal date? | Open a year in the existing chamber-composition chart for original contestant names, seats, chamber percentages and the official seat source. CSV and Notebook entries retain source references. The [Czech timeline](https://hy-central-europe-analysis.org/en/countries/czechia/#es-cz-ps-2010) links the election to its applicable legal version. On the [Hungarian timeline](https://hy-central-europe-analysis.org/en/countries/hungary/#hu-1989-electoral-act), Act XXXIV of 1989 took effect on 30 October 1989 and was repealed from 1 January 2012. The 2010 two-round, 386-seat election uses the earlier law. |

They exercise the election-year links and consecutive navigation, turnout definitions, 200-seat compositions, the zero-seat coalition, identity limits, historical CSV and Notebook, and links between individual elections and the legal timeline. The comparison tests cover 2017/2021, 2010/2021, 2002/2025 and the within-system control 2002/2006. The country overview displayed seven years across two rows on the 390-pixel starting release.
