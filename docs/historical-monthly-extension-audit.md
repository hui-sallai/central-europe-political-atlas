## Definition review, round 2

- **HICP — cleared for the revised all-items series:** `prc_hicp_minr`, `coicop18=TOTAL`, `unit=I15`, NSA. Eurostat states that the ECOICOP-2 classification is applied to the *full* back-series. We independently compared every shared month against archived `prc_hicp_midx`, `coicop=CP00`, `unit=I15`: maximal country-level absolute difference is 0.05 index points (0 for five countries), with no pre-2015 flags. This combination supports all-items continuity for the **current revised series**; it does not assert equality of disaggregated categories or past publication vintages. No legacy series was spliced. [Eurostat HICP metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/prc_hicp_esms.htm).
- **IPI — six country histories cleared, full window not cleared:** `sts_inpr_m`, `PRD`, NACE Rev.2 `B-D`, `SCA`, `I21`. National Eurostat metadata supports the current revised histories from AT 2000, DE 1991, SK 2008, SI 2000, CZ 2000 and HU 2000. PL documents a 2000 series, but its statistical unit changed from LEU to KAU in 2021, so the bridge remains unresolved; RO gives no precise pre-2015 floor. The API contains AT 1996 and SI 1998 values outside their documented national comparability windows. Base-year scale changes alone are not treated as breaks. Per-country source links and dispositions are in `historical_country_definition_review.json`. [Slovak IPI metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/EN/sts_ind_prod_esms_sk.htm); [Polish IPI metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/EN/sts_ind_prod_esms_pl.htm).
- **Unemployment — Czech history cleared, other historical breaks open:** `une_rt_m`, age `TOTAL` (15–74), sex `T`, `PC_ACT`, `SA`. The country-method annex states that Czech data follow the ILO concept from the onset; its unflagged current revised series is cleared from 1993-01. The annex identifies AT/DE/CZ/HU/RO as monthly-LFS inputs and PL/SK/SI as quarterly-LFS-benchmarked series using registered counts as *auxiliary inputs*; the latter are **not** substituted registered-unemployment rates. Germany's TREND is labelled SA in the online series. Hungary introduced state-space monthly estimation from 2023 and revised 2011–2022, leaving the 2009–2010 relationship unresolved. The latest API flags AT 2004-01; DE, SK, SI, HU and PL in 2009; plus other `d` flags. Eurostat says major breaks remain flagged until break-free data are supplied. IESS correction and historical revisions need country-period reconciliation; the 2026 methods annex describes current construction, not proof of unchanged methods since each series began. [Eurostat monthly unemployment metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/une_rt_m_esms.htm); [country-method annex](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/Annexes/une_rt_m_esms_an_Sources_and_methods_for_MUR.pdf).
- **Yield:** `irt_lt_mcby_m`, `MCBY`, monthly `% p.a.`. Romania through 2005-12 and Slovenia through 2003-10 have known primary-market measurement differences; bond-basket and country-source transition history still needs review. No other maturity, policy rate, money-market rate or interpolated substitute was introduced. [Eurostat yield metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/irt_lt_mcby_esms.htm).

## Frozen-shock information support, not inference

These counts use the original JK MP and CBI monthly series and the frozen comparison endpoint 2025-10. Every number before 2015 is a **mathematical-availability preflight** only: it does not establish definition compatibility, balanced estimation eligibility, or coverage. Support units are months, never country-month rows. Potential h=24 clusters use the existing `p_max = ceil((T_eff - 24)^(1/3))`, `p_h = min(h,p_max)` calendar/lag policy; no coefficient is estimated.

The frozen h=0 baseline is **129** time clusters and **86** nonzero MP / **86** nonzero CBI months. For the *unapproved* 2009-01 mathematical scenario, changes versus 2015 are +72 calendar months, +71 potential h=24 clusters and +69 nonzero h=24 months for each shock. Largest-event concentration would fall by .117 for MP and .154 for CBI; top-three concentration by .210 and .109. Those are **not** statistical-validity results and cannot reverse the R2 failure.

Slovenia joined the euro on 2007-01-01; Slovakia joined on 2009-01-01. Earlier observations cannot retroactively enter the fixed Euro-group direct-ECB-policy estimand. For the four non-euro countries, ECB shocks remain external spillovers. Thus 2009-01 is only the earliest *monetary-regime-permitted* Design A date, **not** a definition-cleared start. [ECB Slovenia](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2007/html/pr070102.en.html); [ECB Slovakia](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/changeover/slovakia/html/index.en.html).

Design A (unchanged fixed-group estimand) is a candidate only, with no approved start. Design B (time-varying membership) and Design C (country-specific historical LP) are registered future ideas, not estimated. State-dependent LP remains not started. Crisis/zero-rate/COVID/inflation periods are context markers, never ex-post exclusions. No imputation or backcast was performed by this audit; official historical back-series are preserved as the source publishes them.
