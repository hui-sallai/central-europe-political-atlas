## F. First- and second-round evidence

Round 1 is the national turnout basis: voters who took part divided by all registered voters, as printed.

Round 2 is kept separately in `election_turnout[].round_2`:
- **2002:** 131 constituencies, 6,018,069 registered, 4,423,806 voters, 73.51%.
- **2006:** 110 constituencies, 5,059,002 registered, 3,257,244 voters, 64.39%.

Round-2 votes are a separate ballot (`hu02_smc_round2_vote`, `hu06_smc_round2_vote`) and are never added to round 1. A group's round-2 seats are its final seats minus its round-1 seats (`round2_seats_derived`); the pages print no per-round split.

The registers differ between years:
- 2002 prints no foreign-mission register.
- The 2006 round-2 summary is headed "Tájékoztató adatok" (informational figures). Its counts reproduce its printed turnout.

## G. Statutory versus final allocation

`legislatures[].statutory_tiers` = 176 / 152 / 58 for both years. The final allocation is in `territorial_list_seats` and `national_compensation_seats`, and `tier_transfer.seats` records the seats that moved: 12 in 2002 and 6 in 2006. Under § 8(3), territorial seats left unfilled pass to the national list.

## H. Historical electoral-law versions

Act XXXIV of 1989 stayed in force from 1989-10-30 to 2011-12-31. The provisions that matter for 2002 and 2006 are:

| Provision | Text set by | In force |
|---|---|---|
| § 4: 386 = 176 + 152 + 58 | original 1989 text | 1989-10-30 |
| § 7(3)(a): round-2 entry (15% or the top three) | Act III of 1994 § 3(1) | 1994-01-20 |
| § 8(3): two-thirds rule; leftover territorial seats go to the national list | Act III of 1994 § 4(1) | 1994-01-20 |
| § 8(5): more than 5%; joint list of two parties at least 10%; more than two parties at least 15% | Act XCVI of 1997 § 2 | 1997-11-05 |
| § 9(1): fragment votes | original 1989 text | 1989-10-30 |
| § 9(2)–(6): fragment votes of joint candidates and lists | Act XCVI of 1997 § 3 | 1997-11-05 |
| Annexes 2 and 4: seat table and calculation | Act III of 1994 § 39(3) | 1994-01-20 |

No later amendment of these provisions before 2010 is footnoted. Act XCVIII of 2011 changed § 7(2)(b), § 7(3)(b), § 8(2) and Annex 4 points I and II.1, but only after 2010; the earlier wording of those annex points was not read.

Two other acts were not opened: Act C of 1997 (procedure) and Act LXXXI of 2005. Neither is footnoted on a seat-allocation provision.

There is one wording difference between sources. The 2002 index page says "at least 5%", while the 2006 page and the statute say "more than 5%". No list in 2002 was near 5%, so the outcome does not depend on it, and the statute is followed.
