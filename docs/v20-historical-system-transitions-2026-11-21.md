# Historical electoral systems and Hungary 2010

The starting release was Pages [#453](https://github.com/hui-sallai/central-europe-political-atlas/actions/runs/44794287199), commit `4db61747ee9f792d272e58bc4bb4936da5d4cbab`. That commit admitted the 2010 election and its official national aggregates. The ten-country archive contains 58 chamber-election records. Hungary has five admitted elections, held in 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022 and 2026.

## Reading the 2010 returns

| Observation | Admitted evidence | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| First round, 11 April | 5,172,222 of 8,034,394 registered voters participated (64.38% as printed) | Nationwide electorate. Territorial-list and single-member votes have separate valid-vote totals. |
| Second round, 25 April | 1,160,117 of 2,486,111 (46.66% as printed) | The 57 single-member constituencies still undecided. |
| Final parliamentary seats | 176 single-member + 146 territorial-list + 64 national compensation = 386 | Six seats transferred from the territorial tier to national compensation. The starting territorial maximum was 152 and the national minimum was 58. Two single-member seats went to nominations outside the territorial lists. |
| Contestants | 10 territorial-list organisations, 20 first-round and 6 second-round single-member nominating groups | These 36 ballot rows represent different scopes. Compensation uses fragment votes. The six compensation lists include a zero-seat list without a verified territorial-list match. |

The 2014 election used 199 seats across 106 single-member constituencies and 93 national-list places. Each election retains its own vote and seat denominators, rules and coalition labels. The office's 2010 description supports the recorded 5% and 10% thresholds. The archive does not establish every legal commencement date, candidate-level result or cross-election identity. The [2010 admission record](political-data/hungary_ogy2010_admission.json) and [previous data delivery](v20-historical-gap-closure-2026-11-20.md) identify the official sources and their reuse scope.
