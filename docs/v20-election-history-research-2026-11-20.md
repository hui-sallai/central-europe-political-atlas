# Parliamentary election history and comparison

This report describes the 57-record release. Hungary 2010 was subsequently admitted. The [current historical-system handoff](v20-historical-system-transitions-2026-11-21.md) supersedes the acquisition priorities below.

The starting production release was `600e71a7adbbf7c55a8c5041bf64368ed3fe709f` (Pages run 44660042797). Commit `07ef830` admitted Hungary 2014 and 2018. The next two commits updated coverage and reviewed the layouts. This iteration uses the resulting 57 chamber-election records from ten countries. The [data delivery](v20-historical-backfill-2026-11-20.md) identifies the underlying files, evidence and election rules.

Hungary's 2014, 2018, 2022 and 2026 results cover party and nationality list ballots. Each has 93 national-list seats and 106 constituency seats. In 2018, one of the constituency seats went to an independent. Constituency vote totals and cross-election Hungarian party identities have no admitted series. The archive also includes three Czech elections (2017, 2021 and 2025), one Slovak election (2023) and both Romanian houses for each of 2012, 2016, 2020 and 2024.

## Using the historical views

- The [Politics index](https://hy-central-europe-analysis.org/en/politics/) groups linked polling years by elected chamber. Expand the source details to see available result types and how many elections have dated institutional coverage.
- Open [Hungary 2018 alongside 2022](https://hy-central-europe-analysis.org/en/politics/hungary/?election=el-hu-ogy-2018-04-08&compare=el-hu-ogy-2022-04-03). The historical chart places each turnout observation on its polling date. The seat panel retains independent and nationality representation. Its CSV labels the contestant and allocation-mechanism breakdowns separately.
- The [multi-country history comparison](https://hy-central-europe-analysis.org/en/politics/?history_countries=hungary,czechia,romania&history_from=2014-01-01&history_to=2024-12-31&history_measure=chamber_seats#historical-comparison) supports two to ten countries, a date window and individual elections. A simultaneous bicameral election counts as one polling date and two chamber records. Scope-specific distributions retain their own electorate or seat denominator. The research link, CSV and Notebook item preserve the selection and source references.
- Election-specific rules distinguish dated reforms, their applicable periods and later changes. Each verified party series links to its evidence. The economic handoff opens the selected period and metric in Data Explorer and provides a return link to the originating election.
