# Hungary’s 2002, 2006 and 2010 elections in Political Explorer

## What researchers can compare

The admitted 2006 and 2002 regional-list ballots previously showed blank vote shares for MSZP, alongside 88 and 100 seats with no tier label. The comparison now shows **43.21% and 42.05%**, respectively, computed from the official valid votes on each selected ballot. The 88 and 100 are territorial plus compensation seats. MSZP’s whole-chamber totals were **186 and 178**. Readers can expand the tier breakdown and switch from the nine labels with seats or at least 1% on either ballot to all 24 original labels across this pair. Common spelling alone does not establish a party series.

Each side has its own registered ballot. Links restore independent selections. An unsupported explicit ballot produces a correction prompt. The runoff view gives its polling date, recorded votes and participating constituencies. Published turnout retains two decimals. The 2006 runoff’s official source describes its figures as informational.

| Election | R1 national turnout | R2 turnout and scope | Final constituency / territorial / compensation seats | Territorial seats transferred | Chamber reconciliation |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 2002 | 70.53% | 73.51%, 131 constituencies | 176 / 140 / 70 | 12 | 385 list-linked + 1 MSZP-SZDSZ joint |
| 2006 | 67.83% | 64.39%, 110 constituencies | 176 / 146 / 64 | 6 | 379 list-linked + 6 MSZP-SZDSZ joint + 1 SOMOGYÉRT organisation |
| 2010 | 64.38% | 46.66%, 57 constituencies | 176 / 146 / 64 | 6 | 384 list-linked + 1 FIDESZ-KDNP-MVMP joint + 1 independent |

All three statutory structures specify 176 constituency seats, 152 territorial seats and at least 58 compensation seats. Each final chamber has 386 seats. Transferred mandates explain the different final totals within the same legal period. In 2006, two unmatched compensation records remain inspectable under their original labels. The UI and downloads retain joint, organisation and independent attributions separately. Cross-scope turnout changes are withheld. The election–macro linkage separately retains its recorded 2006-to-2010 national first-round change of −3.45 percentage points.

## Institutional evidence

The three elections link to the period starting 30 October 1989, the 1949 constitution as amended in 1989, and the amendments effective 20 January 1994 and 5 November 1997. Each amendment is nested under the 1989 electoral act and linked to its source. The 2002 and 2006 rules distinguish **more than 5%**, **at least 10% for a two-party joint list**, and **at least 15% for a list of more than two parties**. The 2010 election-rule record gives the 5% and 10% entries. Its empty larger-list entry is a limit of that record. The post-reform comparison continues to distinguish the 386-seat and 199-seat systems.

## Remaining source work

- The historical Hungarian records cover national aggregates. Candidate-level series, cross-election party identities, other amendments and all historical annex versions remain outside the evidence. For the data owner, the 1997 amendment’s archived Chinese summary conflates strict and inclusive thresholds, and the archived pre-2012 pending note predates the two admitted amendments. The presentation uses the registered threshold scopes and dated amendment records. Review the underlying Chinese prose at the next source revision.
- The [admission report](v20-hungary-2002-2006-backfill-2026-11-22.md) distinguishes 30 elections in election–macro research, 64 chamber–election institutional links, and 17 elections in frozen D4.
- The Slovak national list tables for 2002, 2006, 2010, 2012, 2016 and 2020 are already in the elections evidence worktree. For 2020, 2016, 2012 and 2010, obtain the official national participation summaries with registered electors, participants, valid totals, turnout, definitions and provenance.
- The previous limited search for Hungary 1998, 1994 and 1990 found seat pages. All required voting, turnout/register and fragment evidence was not located in that search. Each earlier election also needs its applicable legal version, particularly for elections before the 1997 amendment.
